FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Where does Telemetr.io get its data?

We continuously index public Telegram channels and posts. Channel owners can also connect their channels to share verified private statistics.

How accurate is fake subscriber detection?

Our models analyze subscriber growth patterns, view dynamics and engagement anomalies. They catch the vast majority of bot campaigns and are retrained regularly as fraud tactics change.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever, and all paid plans come with a 7-day trial. No credit card is required to start.

Do you offer an API?

Yes. The REST API is included in the Business plan, and custom volumes are available on request.

Can I add my own channel?

Absolutely. Add your channel for free, verify ownership and get a public media kit you can share with advertisers.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel or change your plan at any time from your account settings. No long-term contracts.
