How it works

From a search request to a channel shortlist

Telemetrio finds channels in its database. Claude or ChatGPT helps you define your criteria, organize the results, and refine your search.

  1. 1

    Describe what you need

    Specify the country, topic, and purpose of your search.

  2. 2

    Review your shortlist

    Explore channels matching your criteria.

  3. 3

    Refine the results

    Change the geography, narrow the category, or ask for a table.

Telemetrio provides the data. Your AI assistant helps you work with it.

FAQ

Questions and answers

What is Telemetrio MCP?

Telemetrio MCP connects Claude or ChatGPT to available Telemetrio data so you can search Telegram channels through conversation.

What can I search for?

Channels matching your geography and category. You can combine criteria and refine them as the conversation continues.

Can I find every Telegram channel?

Search results depend on the channels available in Telemetrio’s database. Some channels may be missing or have incomplete data.

What does a channel’s geography mean?

It refers to the channel’s geographic classification in Telemetrio. It does not mean that every subscriber is located in that country.

Launch dependency: Adjust to the verified geographic data definition. copy.json → faq.geography.verify

Do I need coding skills?

Launch dependency: Write after both connection flows are validated. Only say coding is unnecessary if that is confirmed. FAQ · Do I need coding skills?

How much does it cost?

Launch dependency: Approved Telemetrio access terms (access.json → faqCost). FAQ · How much does it cost?

How current is the data?

Freshness varies by channel and metric. When the source provides an update time, it is shown alongside the data.

Can it automatically buy advertising?

The basic workflow helps you discover channels for further evaluation. Purchasing advertising placements is not included in that workflow.

How do I disconnect Telemetrio?

Launch dependency: Verified disconnect steps for each assistant (connections.json → disconnectSteps). FAQ · How do I disconnect Telemetrio?
